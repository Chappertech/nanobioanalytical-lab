Research

Analytical science across scales.

Our research spans molecular measurement, spectral fingerprinting, advanced materials, environmental analysis and data-driven analytical chemistry.

01

Spectroscopy + AI

Combining vibrational and optical spectroscopy with chemometrics and machine learning for rapid chemical identification, classification and quantification.

02

Chemical Fingerprinting

Developing spectral fingerprints and orthogonal analytical evidence for authentication, provenance, quality assurance and chemical characterisation.

03

Environmental Analysis

Developing analytical strategies for microplastics, contaminants and chemically complex environmental matrices.

04

Nanobioanalytical Materials

Investigating functional and antimicrobial materials at the interface of chemistry, nanotechnology, biology and analytical measurement.

Our Approach

From measurement to evidence.

Analytical signals are only useful when they generate chemically meaningful, reproducible and defensible information. We combine instrumental measurement with quantitative analysis, validation and data science to translate complex signals into evidence.
