Research Programmes

Analytical chemistry for emerging challenges.

Our research programmes are connected by a common objective: developing faster, more informative and more accessible analytical methods for chemically complex systems.

01

AI-Enabled Rapid Analytical Diagnostics

We combine spectroscopy, chemometrics and machine learning to develop rapid analytical platforms for chemical identification, classification and quantitative prediction at the point of need.

02

Circular Materials and Polymer Analytics

We develop analytical methods for recycled and manufactured polymer systems, including material identification, feedstock qualification, contamination detection, degradation assessment and circular-economy decision making.

03

Emerging Pharmaceuticals, Peptides and Sports Integrity

We investigate rapid analytical strategies for emerging pharmaceuticals, peptide-based agents and performance-modifying compounds, integrating rapid screening with complementary confirmatory analysis.

04

AI-Guided Functional Materials and Biointerface Engineering

We combine materials chemistry, advanced characterisation and data-driven modelling to design functional materials and biointerfaces with controlled chemical, antimicrobial and antifouling properties.

Strategic Applications

From laboratory measurement to real-world decisions.

Plastics and the Circular Economy

Developing rapid diagnostic approaches for polymer identification, recycled feedstock quality, contamination and material degradation, with applications across plastic waste recovery and advanced manufacturing.

Emerging Drugs and Sports Integrity

Building analytical capability for rapidly evolving pharmaceuticals, peptides and performance-modifying substances, with relevance to future sports-integrity challenges in the lead-up to Brisbane 2032.

Functional and Antimicrobial Materials

Understanding material-biology interactions and developing functional surfaces and materials designed to control microbial adhesion, biofilm formation and biofouling.

Our Approach

From measurement to evidence.

Analytical signals are only valuable when they provide chemically meaningful, reproducible and defensible information. We integrate instrumental measurement, experimental design, quantitative analysis and data science to convert complex chemical signals into evidence that can support real-world decisions.

1
Define the analytical question
2
Acquire robust chemical measurements
3
Extract informative chemical features
4
Apply chemometrics and machine learning
5
Validate using complementary analytical evidence
6
Translate analytical evidence into decisions
